Flakestake

Flakestake is a dynamic basketball team portfolio showcasing player stats, and match highlights. It blends sleek visuals with interactive features for an immersive experience.

Research

Flakestake aims to explore the intersection of sports and digital media, focusing on the development of basketball team portfolios that enhance fan engagement and attract sponsors. The project investigates how design elements like interactive statistics, player profiles, and visual storytelling can be integrated to create a compelling, user-friendly platform. By researching existing sports portfolios and fan preferences.

Design

Flakestake’s design is built to emphasize the energy and passion of the game. The homepage features bold visuals with an action-packed aesthetic, capturing the essence of basketball through high-quality imagery and dynamic animations. Interactive player profiles highlight key stats and milestones, while the team section showcases past victories and future goals.

Development

Flakestake is a cutting-edge portfolio designed to elevate the online presence of a basketball team. The platform integrates dynamic features such as real-time player statistics, team achievements, match highlights, and upcoming events, offering an engaging and immersive experience for fans, sponsors, and media.

Concept

Flakestake is a cutting-edge basketball team portfolio designed to highlight the essence of a team’s performance, history, and future aspirations. The concept focuses on creating an immersive platform where fans, potential sponsors, and team members can engage with detailed player profiles, game statistics, match highlights, and behind-the-scenes content.
